TEAM TECH

Team Tech is a dynamic, interdisciplinary project group operating under the University of California, Riverside chapter of the Society of Women Engineers (SWE). Our team brings together passionate engineering students from a wide range of disciplines united by a shared commitment to innovation, collaboration, and social impact.

Each year, we partner with an industry sponsor to design and implement an engineering solution that aligns with its humanitarian or socially responsible goals. These projects challenge us to think creatively, apply classroom knowledge in real-world contexts, and develop professional skills in teamwork, project management, and communication.

SmartSpeed | 2026 - 2027
SmartSpeed

SmartSpeed is a project to create multiple systems across campus to measure scooter speed on campus and provide this data to UCPD.

  • Provide accurate data about speed statistics will lead to effective policy development regarding e-scooters.
  • Design and build a system powered by a Raspberry Pi with computer vision capabilities, speed measuring sensors, and renewable solar energy.
  • Use computer vision classification algorithms to identify which type of entity (pedestrian, bike, or scooter) triggered the sensors, and save the speed and classification type to a database.
  • Recreate this system and place multiple systems in high traffic areas across campus suggested by UCPD.
  • Integrate solar energy to ensure the device operates sustainably.
SOLARA | 2025 - 2026
SOLARA (Sunlight-Oriented Learning and Research Analyzer)

SOLARA is a semi-autonomous, solar-powered rover built to map R'Garden and collect environmental data, giving farmers data-driven recommendations to maximize produce output.

  • Custom CAD chassis with solar power integration
  • SE350 edge server data pipeline for real-time processing
  • Trained ML models for environmental analysis
  • Personalized web dashboard with live data visualizations
  • Custom companion app for farmer recommendations
  • Integrated multi-sensor suite (soil, climate, imaging)
UPS System Modification | 2024 - 2025
UPS (Uninterrupted Power Supply) System Modification

Created a modification of a UPS system for the Lenovo Thinkserver with a competing zinc-manganese dioxide battery

  • Research and development of a zinc-manganese dioxide battery that can compete with lithium-ion batteries in terms of energy density and lifespan.
  • Modification of the UPS system to accommodate the new battery design, ensuring compatibility and optimal performance.
  • Implementation of safety measures to ensure the battery is non-flammable, utilizing potassium hydroxide as the electrolyte.
  • Evaluation of the environmental impact of lithium mining and promotion of sustainable alternatives through the use of zinc-manganese dioxide batteries.
Pi Tech Design Alternatives | 2022 - 2023
Pi Tech Design Alternatives

Research second sourced embedded computers for a viable alternative(s) to Raspberry Pi and then come up with a technical design of such alternative.

  • Develop a network of various physical devices that are able to collect, share, store, and visualize environmental sensor data from various types of locations.
  • Team members gain hands-on experience working with sensors and handling sensor data.
  • Learn new skills, for example this year we utilized technologies such as PowerBi, MQTT network protocols, sensor driver and script writing using Python.
Pi Environmetal Data | 2021 - 2022
Pi Environmental Data

Collaborating with the cloud computing company, Hyve Solutions, to build a network of sensors and Raspberry Pi’s to autonomously collect and analyze environmental data.

  • Through weekly meetings, members were able to learn how to program in Python and a Linux environment, use Github, and use the MQTT network protocol.
  • Members learned how to create a basic MQTT network in Python, while awaiting the Raspberry Pi’s and sensors to eventually arrive.
  • Completing a functioning simple subscribe and publish MQTT network, which allows for data to be sent to and received from a remote dashboard.
Manufacturing Error Detection | 2020 - 2021
Manufacturing Error Detection

Collaborating with the cloud computing company, Hyve Solutions,use AI-based machine learning to be able to identify manufacturing errors.

  • Use AI-based machine learning to be able to identify manufacturing errors such as the following: cable management, broken or unplugged cable connections, label recognition, and label placement.
  • Members learned about coding in python and Linux, communicate with professionals in the industry, work with raspberry pis to create Artificial Intelligence, test out different cameras and lenses.

Browser-Based DJ Mixer
